GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is SheetPDF free to use?＋

Yes. The tools included in this demo are free to try. There is no account or payment step.

Can every PDF be converted perfectly to Excel?＋

No. This first version works best with text-based PDFs. Complex tables, multi-column layouts, and scanned pages may need manual cleanup or OCR.

Are my files uploaded?＋

The working converters in this demo process supported files locally in your browser. This website does load third-party JavaScript libraries, so review your privacy needs before using sensitive files.

Why does my converted PDF look different?＋

Excel-to-PDF export uses a simplified table layout. Very wide or heavily formatted workbooks may need adjustments before printing.

How do I report a problem?＋

Add your business email to the Contact page before publishing this website. The demo does not yet have a connected support inbox.
